# Okulo Studio

Websites made by people who look twice.

We're a two-person studio, Kriz Bacus and Sean Relampagos, in the Philippines, designing and building animated, grid-led websites and brand identities for companies that want to be more creative than their industry expects. Okulo comes from oculus, the eye.

## How we work

You work directly with the two people doing the work, from the first sketch to the last line of code. Every layout is drawn by hand and every motion is timed by eye, on a grid. We design the brand and build the site, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

We work worldwide, in English, on Manila time (GMT+8).
